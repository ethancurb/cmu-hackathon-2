The seeded search uses a $1,200 personal monthly rent cap, two equal shares as an editable assumption, 2 bedrooms, at least 2 advertised bathrooms, and a computed walk of at most 20 minutes to the mapped Gates Hillman entrance. Utilities, fees, transit, nearby essentials, coverage gaps and meaningful alternatives are explicit parts of the design. The morning readiness target is approximately 11 a.m. EDT on September 12, 2026.
