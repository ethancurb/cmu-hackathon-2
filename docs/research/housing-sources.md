## Concrete evidence and semantic limits

These Oakland/Shadyside leads are not route-qualified. CMU’s [One on Centre](https://offcampus.housing.cmu.edu/city/pittsburgh-pa/listing/one-on-centre-47925) is North Oakland: 2x2, 829 sqft, “Call For Price,” availability “Ask,” base rent and extra fees excluded; displayed commute is 9 minutes to “The Oval,” not Gates Hillman. Kerpec’s [514 Ivy Street](https://www.kerpecmgt.com/514-ivy-street-shadyside-apartments) is Shadyside: 2bd $1,595/month, available 8/1/2026, gas heat/central air, bath count/utilities unstated. Lobos’ [Bentley Apartments](https://lobosmanagement.com/units/bentley-apartments-021-c-02/) is Shadyside: 2bd/1ba, $1,899/month, available now; “heat” is an amenity, inclusion unstated. Craigslist [636 Maryland Avenue](https://pittsburgh.craigslist.org/apa/d/pittsburgh-classic-shadyside-living/7928045095.html) is Shadyside: 2bd/1.5ba, $795/month, 1,200 sqft, dated April 15, 2026; availability/utilities unstated. All need current confirmation and Gates Hillman routing.

For contrast, [Waldorf Park B-206](https://lobosmanagement.com/units/waldorf-park-apts-045-b-206/) is Ross Township, not Oakland/Shadyside: $1,500, 2bd/2ba, available now; utilities unknown and unlikely walk-qualified.

None proves a 20-minute walk to a verified Gates Hillman entrance. Ingestion must geocode the exact address and route to the selected entrance, preserving route date/mode/assumptions. A building summary is not proof that a particular unit is vacant.

Overlap is substantial: Oak Hill, The Julian, New King Edward, The Lofts at Terrain and Waldorf Park recur across Zillow Group pages, Redfin, Realtor.com, Apartments.com, Zumper and Apartment List. Zillow’s footer links Trulia and HotPads; Redfin’s public rentals material identifies the Rent.com/Apartment Guide marketplace relationship. Counts cannot be summed; reconcile by address/building, unit, floor plan, manager, price basis and observation time.

## Best feasible initial strategy

For the seeded demo, use CMU’s marketplace and direct Oakland/Shadyside managers as research leads, then pursue source-approved access for any connector. Zumper and Apartments.com are especially useful for commute/utility and fee semantics; managers/CMU must verify availability, bath count and utilities. Treat Rent.com, ApartmentGuide, PadMapper and Facebook as unproven/unavailable. Attach source URL, evidence channel, timestamp, listing-vs-unit identity, price basis, lease/availability evidence and utility status to every record.

The hardest risks are (1) identity/duplication across shared feeds and building summaries versus a unit, (2) price semantics—base rent, required fees, concessions, per-bed versus whole-unit and “total monthly price”—and (3) utility/time truth: missing utilities are unknown, while “available now,” stale posts and future lease dates can conflict. A fourth is routing: “near CMU” or portal campus minutes is not the required Gates Hillman pedestrian route.
