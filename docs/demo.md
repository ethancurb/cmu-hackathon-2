# 90-second demo

Start with `npm run demo:reset`, run the built server with `npm start`, and open
`http://127.0.0.1:4173`. The walkthrough assumes the browser is reset to the
original search.

1. **0:00–0:10 — establish the question.** Show the workspace header and the
   criteria bar: `$1,200` for your share, two equal shares (assumed), `2 beds ·
   2+ baths`, a 20-minute walk, and the Gates Hillman mapped entrance.

2. **0:10–0:25 — show the evidence.** Browse the list and map together. Point
   out that the seed has 49 options and that the exact-match count is zero.
   Open one row to show the source URL, observation time, rent basis, and
   utility states. Unknown utilities are unresolved evidence, not free costs.

3. **0:25–0:42 — show a small money change.** Open Alternatives and apply the
   `$7.50` personal-cap increase. Webster Hall B2 appears as a match at
   `$2,415` whole-home rent, or `$1,207.50` per person under the two-share
   assumption. Explain that the original requirement remains visible.

4. **0:42–0:57 — show a layout compromise.** Revert, then change the minimum
   baths to 1. Schenley House appears at `$1,995` whole-home rent with one
   advertised bath. Its deviation is visible in the result rather than hidden
   in a score.

5. **0:57–1:12 — show a daily-life compromise.** Revert and use the walking
   alternative for 6350 Forward. It keeps the 2BR/2BA and `$1,995` whole-home
   rent while accepting the longer computed walk. Select it on the map to
   connect the home to the Gates Hillman destination.

6. **1:12–1:30 — close on action and coverage.** Save one or two homes to the
   shortlist, select Compare, and open Coverage. Show the three imported
   organizations, retrieval dates, route/context counts, and remaining source
   gaps. Mention **Find more homes** for bounded live research and **Check a
   listing** for one public URL a renter wants verified.

Keep the framing concrete: the application carries the fragmented search
burden, preserves what each source actually said, and makes the cost, layout,
and walking compromises inspectable. A saved snapshot is reliable for the
walkthrough, but its listing observation is not a promise of current vacancy.
