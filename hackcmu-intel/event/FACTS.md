## Source discipline

- **Official fact:** stated in the supplied deck or a current organizer publication; record the source.
- **Observed result:** a specific test or inspection, bounded by its time and sample.
- **Team preference:** the user's execution choice, not an organizer rule.
- **Recommendation:** our inference about building or competing effectively.
- **Unknown:** not established; do not silently promote it into a restriction or permission.

Check dated organizer updates when a consequential fact changes. The PDF and public pages are complementary, not interchangeable. If official sources conflict, record both and resolve only the affected decision. Continue unrelated authorized work. An agent must not independently message organizers without the human's authorization.

## Main-track fit

| Main track | A useful way to demonstrate fit |
